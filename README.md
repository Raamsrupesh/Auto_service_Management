**Problem Statement**: Smart Shared Transport Management and Optimisation Platform for College Commuters

In many colleges located outside urban areas, students rely on monthly shared auto services for their daily commute. These transport services are usually managed through informal coordination between students and auto drivers, leading to inefficient passenger allocation, unnecessary waiting time, poor seat utilisation, and operational challenges for drivers.

Although students subscribe to fixed monthly transport services, the existing system lacks real-time visibility into passenger availability. Students who arrive early at the pickup point often have to wait for other passengers, while available autos may not be utilised efficiently. Auto drivers also face difficulties in managing passenger groups, balancing trips, and ensuring fair revenue distribution among multiple transport providers.

The proposed system aims to build a smart shared transport management platform that enables subscribed students and registered auto drivers to coordinate efficiently. Using real-time availability tracking, destination-based passenger grouping, intelligent auto allocation, and fair revenue management, the platform reduces passenger waiting time, improves vehicle utilisation, and creates a transparent transport ecosystem.

The system leverages location-based services, real-time communication, and automated allocation algorithms to dynamically match available students with suitable autos while maintaining fairness among transport providers.



SMART SHARED TRANSPORT MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

**FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS**

1. User Authentication
- Students, auto drivers, and admin should be able to register and log in.
- The system should provide role-based access according to the user type.

2. Subscription Management
- Students should be able to join and manage their monthly auto subscription.
- The system should maintain subscription details and validity.

3. Student Availability Tracking
- Students should be able to mark their availability after college.
- The system can also use location-based services to detect student availability.

4. Destination-Based Grouping
- Students travelling towards the same destination should be grouped together.
- The system should manage different routes and destinations.

5. Auto Allocation System
- The system should assign available students to suitable autos based on destination, seat capacity, and availability.
- It should prevent overbooking of autos.

6. Trip Management
- The system should maintain daily trip details, passenger assignments, and travel records.

7. Revenue Management
- The system should track subscription payments and support fair revenue distribution among auto drivers.

8. Notification System
- Students and auto drivers should receive notifications regarding auto assignments, trip updates, and delays.


**NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS**

1. Performance
- The system should provide quick auto allocation and real-time updates without delays.

2. Scalability
- The system should support increasing numbers of students, auto drivers, routes, and colleges.

3. Security
- User information, payment details, and location data should be securely protected.

4. Privacy
- Student location information should only be accessed when required and with proper permission.

5. Reliability
- The system should provide accurate auto assignments and prevent incorrect allocations.

6. Availability
- The application should remain available and work smoothly during peak college travel hours.

7. Usability
- The system should be simple and easy to use for students, auto drivers, and administrators.

8. Maintainability
- The system should have a modular design so that new features can be added easily in the future.
